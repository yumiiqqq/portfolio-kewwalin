Recipe: Cinnamon Roll Wreath

🍒

Ingredients

  • Crescent roll dough (sheets recommended)
  • Pazazz apples (sweet, tart, and extra crispy)
  • Unsalted butter (softened at room temp)
  • Granulated sugar & Brown sugar
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Cream cheese (softened 30 mins)
  • Milk & Vanilla extract
  • Powdered sugar

Baking Tips

  • Dough: Sheets of dough are easier to work with because you don’t need to press perforations together.
  • Butter & Cream Cheese: Remove from the refrigerator at least 30 minutes before starting.

Cinnamon Roll Wreath

Step by step instructions

  • Roll out: Place both sheets slightly overlapping on parchment paper, press seams together.
  • Top with butter: Combine softened butter with sugars and cinnamon. Spread evenly.
  • Add apples: Core and thinly slice one apple, place in an even layer. Do not overlap.
  • Roll it up: Roll tightly over apples into a tube.
  • Slice in half: Slice lengthwise through the middle. Make sure apples face up.
  • Braid & Form: Braid the two halves together, form a wreath. Bake at 350°F for 20-25 mins.
